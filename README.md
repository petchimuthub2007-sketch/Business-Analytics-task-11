# Week 11 – Business Analysis using Bubble Chart

## 📌 Objective
Analyze and compare different business categories using a Bubble Chart in Tableau.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Tableau Public
- CSV Dataset
- GitHub

## 📊 Visualizations
- Bubble Chart – Monthly Sales by Department
- Bar Chart – Sales by Department
- Pie Chart – Sales Contribution by City
- Line Chart – Monthly Sales Trend

## 🎛️ Filter
- Department Filter

## 💡 Business Insights
- Sales performance varies across departments.
- High-sized bubbles represent departments with higher sales.
- Some cities contribute more to overall sales.
- Sales vary across different joining months.
- The dashboard makes category comparison easier.

## 💼 Business Recommendations
- Focus resources on high-performing departments and cities.
- Improve strategies for low-performing departments to increase sales.

## ✅ Conclusion
The Bubble Chart and supporting visualizations provide a clear comparison of business performance and help identify important high and low performing categories.
